# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- `Graph.Root`, `Graph.Canvas`, `Graph.Bands`, `Graph.Connectors`, `Graph.Markers`, `Graph.Nodes`, `Graph.Node`, and
  the hooks `useGraph()` and `useNode()`.
- Fixed or measured node sizes; growing to the right or down; curved, right-angled or straight connectors.
- One lane open at a time or several; controlled or uncontrolled open lanes and selection; route tracing on hover and
  focus; arrow-key navigation.
- `treelines/core`: `layout`, `connectorPath`, `trace`, `onTrace`, `foldPassThrough` and `fromTree`, without React.
